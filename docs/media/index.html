Press enquiries

I am happy to comment on:

  • the IMF, its lending programmes, and the conditions attached to them;
  • austerity, social protection, and health in low- and middle-income countries;
  • climate change, crisis finance, and the international financial institutions;
  • African politics, including elections in East Africa and conflict in the Sahel.

My work has been covered by The Guardian, The Washington Post, Al Jazeera, Agence France-Presse, Radio New Zealand, and the New Zealand Herald, among others.

Email thomas.stubbs@rhul.ac.uk.
