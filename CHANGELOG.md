## 17 November 2026: the rebuilt site goes live

The first release of tstubbs.net as a static Quarto site on GitHub Pages, replacing the Owlstown site at www.tstubbs.net. Its source, with full history, is in the public repository thomasairbag/tstubbs-website.

- Pages: Home, About, Books, Academic Writing, Policy & Advisory, Media, Teaching, CV, and Contact. The menu (About, Books, Academic Writing, Policy & Advisory, Media, Teaching, CV) shows in full from 992px wide, with email and four profile links beside it.
- Publications come from Thomas's Zotero library, so each is edited in one place, with Zotero's APA citations: 45 articles, commentaries, chapters, and working papers on Academic Writing, 14 policy reports on Policy & Advisory, and 20 pieces of public writing on Media, each by year, and the two books on Books. 60 of them have their PDF on the site, and every item has a DOI or a link.
- Where they apply, entries carry a Data button for replication data (16 works), an award line (3), and "Cited in" lines naming the policy documents that cite them (123 lines on 28 works), with long lists folded.
- Books: both books with their covers and descriptions, and *Greening the International Monetary Fund* with its open-access PDF; *A Thousand Cuts* also with praise, the journals that reviewed it, and the documents citing it.
- Home: the portrait, posts, introduction, and a box on IMF Monitor; areas of expertise; the two books; the three newest publications. About: biography, research in three strands with selected work, policy engagement, and service. Teaching: modules, doctoral supervision, and a note for prospective PhD students.
- The CV is one PDF at a fixed address, shown on the CV page (download only on phones), and kept current from Thomas's own copy.
- The domain: tstubbs.net works without `www` for the first time, `www.tstubbs.net` redirects to it, and every page is served over HTTPS.
- Old addresses: Academic Writing and CV keep their Owlstown addresses (`/publications/` and `/cv/`). Every other old address, such as a single publication's page, shows the "page not found" page, which links to Home, Academic Writing, and Contact; the site has no redirects.
- Design: Fraunces for headings and IBM Plex Sans for text, both self-hosted, with an oxblood accent.
- Accessibility (WCAG 2.2 AA): a skip link, visible keyboard focus, alt text, layouts for phones, and checks with axe. Phones don't turn dates and numbers into phone-number links.
- Each page has its own search description, and every page carries Google Analytics, reporting to the existing Analytics property. The sitemap is submitted to Google Search Console.
